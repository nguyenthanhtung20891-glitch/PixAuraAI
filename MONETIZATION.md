# Trial, subscription and lifetime licensing

Commercial baseline: 14-day free trial, $0.99/month subscription, $19.99 lifetime purchase. Prices are reference USD values; localized store prices/tax and approved storefront configuration must be verified during Phase 10. No consumable AI credit design for local editing. Model packs necessary for licensed MVP tools do not require per-edit payment.

Use StoreKit 2 on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android. Plan a local app-access trial independent of store introductory-offer availability; final implementation must verify store policy and subscription-offer constraints before release. Store subscription free-trial offers must not be assumed to support exactly fourteen days everywhere. Record a receipt-derived verified entitlement and monotonic elapsed-time evidence when available; detect clock rollback without deleting or locking user work.

Manual/local editing remains available for the 14-day trial. After expiry, new editing requires subscription or lifetime entitlement, but existing projects can always be viewed and the last approved revision exported for recovery. Subscription checks cannot interrupt an in-progress edit/export. Verified lifetime entitlement works offline indefinitely. Subscription uses last verified expiry plus proposed seven-day offline grace; final grace is a Phase 10 ADR after fraud/usability evaluation. Trial begins on first successful edit, not installation. Reinstall trial abuse is an acknowledged offline limitation; do not introduce invasive identifiers to solve it.

Restore purchases, pending payments, cancellation, refund/revocation and family-sharing policy need explicit tests. Cached entitlement is tamper-resistant through platform secure storage, not claimed unbreakable. No account/server required to edit. Cross-platform purchase sharing is outside MVP unless a later account/product decision adds it. Receipt/network traffic contains purchase identifiers only, never photo data. Human-owned store accounts, tax contracts and signing keys are release gates.

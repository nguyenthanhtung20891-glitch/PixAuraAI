# Trial, Free tier, subscription and lifetime licensing

Active approved policy: ADR0024, Product Owner decision2026-11-15. This supersedes
older trial-only paywall/proposed-grace descriptions; certified historical records
remain unchanged. Prices are reference USD values, not invented store configuration.

Fourteen calendar days start at first successful committed edit, anchored UTC; trial
users edit and use AI without Free daily quotas. Preview, failure and cancellation
never start it. After trial, Free users have five distinct immutable photo sources/day
shared across opening/importing for editing, editing and ordinary export, plus three
successful valid usable AI proposal deliveries/day. Reopening, editing, previewing,
Undo/Redo or exporting today's admitted source again consumes no new photo slot.
Multi-action AI proposals count once; malformed/failed/resource-denied/cancelled/stale
requests and redisplay do not charge. Future voice activation/transcription/dialogue
never independently consumes editing quota; Phase14 is not implemented.

Daily reset is00:00UTC with local-time equivalent shown. Monotonic elapsed evidence,
durable UTC high-water and uncertainty handling prevent untrusted clock/timezone
changes granting extra resets. After reboot without trusted time, retain high-water,
accumulate proved uptime and explain uncertainty; no fabricated offline anti-abuse.
Trial/subscription time-based grants require trusted/proved time; lifetime and recovery
remain available. Reinstall/deletion/compromised-device limits are acknowledged without
invasive fingerprinting. See ADR0024 for transactional reservations and clock rules.

Monthly referenceUSD0.99 and lifetimeUSD19.99 remove both daily quotas. Subscription
offline grace ends at last verified expiry+7days, never extends on verification failure.
Verified lifetime remains offline indefinitely, subject to authoritative refund/revocation.
StoreKit2 / Play Billing and proper authenticated verification are required. No unverified
flags, mocks or development observation envelopes are store transactions. Human-owned
product IDs, prices/tax, contracts/accounts and real sandbox tests are prerequisites.

Saved content always stays inspectable/recoverable; export of the last approved saved
revision bypasses quota/entitlement/cache failure. Never delete or lock user work or
interrupt an already authorized edit/export. No PixAuraAI account solely to edit. A
minimal Android transaction metadata verifier is allowed; no photos, voice, conversation,
history, analytics, source quotas or user-identification collection on that server.
Local quota state is private/no-backup, bounded and separate from project Schema3.
Native HUMAN Apply/ownership/history remain authoritative, not a license or quota token.

Implementation status: these are approved requirements, not a claim that the current
application enables checkout or quota enforcement. Independently tested components
are submitted in docs/reports/phase-11-g8-g10-engineering-v13.md; versioned native
receipt/inbox integration and genuine store qualification remain acceptance gates.
